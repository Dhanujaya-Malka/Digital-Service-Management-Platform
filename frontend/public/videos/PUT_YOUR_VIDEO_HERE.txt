========================================================================
V2NEXUS - HOW TO ADD YOUR VIDEO TUTORIAL
========================================================================

Obe video file eka me folder ekata copy karanna:

  Path: frontend/public/videos/

Nam thiyanna puluwan widi:
  1. tutorial.mp4 (Recommended)
     file: frontend/public/videos/tutorial.mp4

  2. how-to-connect.mp4
     file: frontend/public/videos/how-to-connect.mp4

Obe video eka methanata "tutorial.mp4" kiyala dammaha website eke "How it works"
page eke auto play wenna setup karala thiyenne.

Thawa YouTube video ekak unath embed karanna puluwan widiyata How It Works
page eke code eka hadala thiyenawa.
